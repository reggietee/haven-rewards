### 1. Sponsor

The “Spin Your Way to Haven” contest (the **Contest**) is sponsored and administered by Haven Workspace (the **Sponsor**), 242 Mary St, Unit 8, Niagara-on-the-Lake, Ontario, Canada. Questions about the Contest may be sent to info@havenworkspace.ca.

Certain prizes are supplied by Zannes Law Firm and the Niagara Falls Innovation Hub. Those organizations are not responsible for administering the Contest except as expressly stated in these Rules.

### 2. Contest period and location

The Contest begins at 3:00 p.m. Eastern Time on September 22, 2026 and closes at 7:00 p.m. Eastern Time on September 22, 2026 (the **Contest Period**).

Entry is available in person at the Haven Workspace booth at NFIH Demo Day, 4551 Zimmerman Avenue, Niagara Falls, Ontario. The Sponsor's contest device is the official timekeeping device. No entries or spins will be accepted after the Contest closes.

### 3. Eligibility

The Contest is open to legal residents of Canada who are 18 years of age or older when they enter and who attend NFIH Demo Day in person.

The Contest is not open to:

- owners, officers, employees, representatives, or contractors directly involved in administering the Contest for Haven Workspace, Zannes Law Firm, or the Niagara Falls Innovation Hub;
- members of the immediate household of any person listed above;
- anyone whose Haven Workspace membership has previously been cancelled by Haven Workspace or who has previously been refused access to Haven Workspace; or
- anyone otherwise ineligible under applicable law.

The Sponsor may require proof of age, identity, residency, and eligibility before confirming a prize.

### 4. How to enter

No purchase is necessary. A purchase will not increase the chance of winning.

To enter during the Contest Period, an eligible entrant must:

1. attend the Haven Workspace booth in person;
2. enter their own first and last name and email address on the contest device;
3. confirm that they are at least 18 years old and agree to these Rules; and
4. complete one spin on the contest device.

There is a limit of one entry and one spin per person during the Contest Period. Entries made using multiple email addresses, on behalf of another person, using false information, or by any automated or fraudulent method are void. A duplicate email address will be blocked. Entries that appear to use a different email address for the same person may be flagged for review.

Joining the Haven.fm waitlist is optional, is not required to enter or win, and does not affect an entrant's chance of winning. The waitlist choice is presented as a separate, unchecked opt-in.

### 6. How potential prizes are selected

The wheel animation is a visual presentation of the result selected by the Contest's prize engine. The apparent size, position, repetition, or arrangement of a prize on the visual wheel does not represent the mathematical odds of receiving that prize.

The 53 finite featured prize units are divided approximately evenly among four release windows: 3:00–4:00 p.m., 4:00–5:00 p.m., 5:00–6:00 p.m., and 6:00–7:00 p.m. Each featured prize unit is assigned a release moment within its applicable window using a random process. After that moment occurs, the next eligible completed spin may receive that featured prize. If more than one featured prize is available, the prize awarded is selected randomly from the available units. A featured prize that is not awarded in its release window carries forward and remains available in the following window. Any featured prize not awarded by 7:00 p.m. will not be awarded.

Each featured prize can be awarded no more than the number of times shown in section 5. When no released featured prize is available for a completed spin, the entrant receives a potential 1-Day Coworking Pass.

The chance of receiving a featured prize, or any particular featured prize, cannot be stated in advance. It varies based on the number and timing of eligible entries, the randomly generated prize release moments, and the number and type of featured prizes remaining. Between approximately 100 and 200 entries are expected, but actual participation may be higher or lower.

The contest screen may display the total number of featured prizes remaining. It will not reveal future release moments.

### 7. Potential winner status and skill-testing question

The result shown on the wheel is a **potential prize** and is not final until the entrant:

- is confirmed eligible;
- has complied with these Rules;
- correctly answers, without assistance or a calculator, a time-limited mathematical skill-testing question administered by the Sponsor; and
- completes any approval, conflict-check, identification, documentation, or agreement requirements that apply to the prize.

The Sponsor will contact potential winners using the email address supplied at entry, normally within three business days after the Contest. A potential winner must respond, attend Haven Workspace if requested, provide the reasonably required eligibility or prize information, complete the skill-testing question, and complete any applicable approval steps within 30 calendar days after the Sponsor's notice. Failure to do so, failure to answer the skill-testing question correctly, or failure to satisfy the applicable eligibility and prize conditions may result in disqualification and forfeiture.

The Sponsor may, but is not required to, award a forfeited featured prize to another eligible entrant selected randomly from the remaining eligible entries, subject to the same conditions.

### 8. Approval-based prizes

Memberships, the Niagara Passport, and the Business Mailing Address prize are subject to Haven Workspace's standard approval process, which may include coming to Haven Workspace within the 30-day claim period, completing a short meeting or tour, providing the requested information, and signing the applicable member agreement. The Business Mailing Address prize also requires the same identification and business documentation Haven Workspace requires from its regular clients.

If a potential winner does not pass the applicable approval process, the prize will be forfeited. Haven Workspace is not required to disclose confidential safety, community, legal, or business reasons for an approval decision, subject to applicable law.

### 9. Zannes Law Firm prizes

Zannes Law Firm prizes are subject to the firm's conflict check, service availability, professional obligations, and engagement terms. Being shown or contacted about a potential legal-services prize does not create a lawyer-client relationship. A lawyer-client relationship begins only if and when Zannes Law Firm confirms it in writing.

### 10. Booking coworking passes

Each confirmed 1-Day Coworking Pass and 5-Day Pass Pack will receive a unique code linked to the winner's name and email address.

Passes may be booked only for available Tuesdays and Thursdays, from 9:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m., between October 1 and December 17, 2026. The initial capacity is four prize bookings per day, but the Sponsor may add or remove available dates or adjust daily capacity based on workspace operations. Availability is first come, first served.

Each day in a 5-Day Pass Pack must be booked separately. A winner may cancel or change a booking before the booked day, subject to availability. All unused pass days expire after December 17, 2026.

The name on a booking must match the name on the contest entry. Identification may be required at check-in. Codes and bookings cannot be transferred.

### 11. Other prize use

- Membership prizes must begin within 30 days after approval, unless the Sponsor agrees otherwise in writing.
- Private Office prizes require advance booking, are subject to availability, and subject to reasonable fulfillment terms supplied to the winner.
- The Haven Event + Coworking Day Bundle includes one ticket worth up to $20 for a Haven-hosted event occurring within 12 months after the Contest, subject to capacity and any event-specific restrictions, plus one coworking day subject to the booking terms in section 10.
- Niagara Passport, Story Mode, and other third-party prizes are subject to the applicable provider's reasonable fulfillment terms, which will be provided to the potential winner.

### 12. Personal information and Haven.fm opt-in

The Sponsor collects each entrant's name, email address, eligibility confirmation, contest activity, and related fulfillment information to administer the Contest, prevent duplicate entries, contact potential winners, fulfill prizes, maintain records, and comply with legal obligations.

Contest information will not be used to send marketing emails unless the entrant separately and expressly opts in to the Haven.fm waitlist. The optional waitlist consent request identifies the sender, explains the messages the entrant agrees to receive, provides contact information, and explains that consent may be withdrawn at any time. The Sponsor will retain a record of the wording shown, the entrant's choice, the date and time, and the source of the consent.

Entrants may contact info@havenworkspace.ca about their information or to withdraw marketing consent. Withdrawing marketing consent does not affect contest eligibility or an awarded prize.

The Sponsor may use service providers to host the contest, store and synchronize entry records, and support prize administration. The Sponsor will limit access to personal information to people and providers who need it for those purposes, subject to its privacy practices and applicable law.

### 13. Technical problems and event changes

The Sponsor may pause, modify, suspend, or cancel the Contest if fraud, tampering, equipment failure, loss of connectivity, weather, venue conditions, or another event outside its reasonable control affects the security, fairness, integrity, or proper operation of the Contest.

The contest device may temporarily store entries and outcomes offline and synchronize them when connectivity returns. An entrant is responsible for reviewing their information before submitting it. The Sponsor is not responsible for entries that are incomplete, inaccurate, lost, corrupted, or not received because of circumstances outside its reasonable control, but will use reasonable efforts to preserve valid entries recorded by the contest device.

If a technical or human error causes more potential prize notices to be displayed than there are prizes available, the number of prizes listed in section 5 remains the maximum. The Sponsor will resolve affected claims fairly and randomly from among eligible claimants, subject to applicable law.

### 14. Conduct and disqualification

The Sponsor may disqualify an entrant who tampers with the Contest, attempts to obtain more than one spin, uses false or misleading information, interferes with another entrant, acts abusively toward staff or attendees, or otherwise violates these Rules.

Any attempt to undermine the legitimate operation of the Contest may violate civil or criminal law. The Sponsor reserves the right to seek remedies permitted by law.

### 15. Release and limitation of liability

By entering, each entrant agrees, to the extent permitted by law, to release and hold harmless the Sponsor, prize providers, venue, event organizer, and each of their respective owners, directors, officers, employees, and representatives from claims arising from participation in the Contest or acceptance or use of a prize, except for liability that cannot lawfully be excluded.

Nothing in these Rules limits any right or remedy that cannot be limited under applicable law.

### 16. Decisions, governing law, and conflict

The Sponsor's decisions concerning the administration of the Contest are final, subject to applicable law. The Contest and these Rules are governed by the laws of Ontario and the federal laws of Canada applicable in Ontario.

If there is a conflict between these Rules and any short-form contest notice or promotional material, these Rules govern, subject to applicable law.
