## Design Context
Audience: Canadian adult attendees at Haven’s NFIH Demo Day booth, using one iPad Air in landscape outdoors under a tent.
Purpose: a quick, private entry and an exciting potential prize reveal. Staff need reliable offline inventory and exports.
Brand voice: warm, assured, theatrical. The explicit brief calls for navy, violet/electric-blue atmosphere, gold metal, generous spacing, calm form, spectacular wheel.
Typography: use Manrope for clear generous forms and Oxanium for the game-show headline and prize numerals; avoid decorative type in legal text.
Principles: large accessible controls; wheel as a physical stage object; no sensitive state after reset; spectacle scales to value; all assets local.

Home composition: a natural scrolling page with a 56% form / 44% real 3D wheel hero. Calm 90-second idle rotation; one Canvas persists into the recorded spin. Full-width dark metallic editorial Prize Vault below, followed by the natural footer. Thirteen prize types grouped by explicit Platinum, Gold, Silver and Bronze presentation fields; never infer metal from value or celebration intensity. Written labels, distinct emblems and material treatments support grayscale recognition. Portrait stacks form, wheel, gallery. No nested prize/form scrolling or pale field-like prize cards.
